## Deliberate uncertainties

- **VBC:** the official timetable says opens 15 November, starts applications by 8 January and closes 11 January, but does not state the year. We do not manufacture a 2027 deadline from this annual pattern. Status stays unknown.
- **ETH:** the programme dates page says noon CET; the 9 October 2026 news post says noon GMT. The shared date is stored, with no fabricated exact timestamp. Confirm with the organiser.
- **NUS:** the dated 2027 timeline says 1 November 2026, while another paragraph says 1 November 2027. Status stays unknown. The timeline’s published closing date and time are recorded separately.
- **UNIL:** the wording is “midnight CET” on 15 November. The date is recorded without inventing a 23:59 minute-level cut-off.
- **KI:** a historical 2026 cycle is retained to show its actual published conditions. These must not be carried into an unannounced 2027 cycle.
- Fields marked **Not yet checked** have not been researched sufficiently; **Not stated by the source** refers only to the page actually checked. Neither means that a requirement or cost is absent.

The separate fictional fixtures are for UI exploration and regressions. Real and demo records use separate collection controls and visible badges, including in saved opportunities. Do not present demo assertions as factual claims.
